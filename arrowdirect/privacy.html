About the Application

Arrow Direct is a polished, offline-first arrow maze puzzle game for Android. Players tap an arrow to shoot it out of the board while avoiding collisions with other arrows.

🎮 Local Processing & Persistence:

All game logic, progression, and high scores are processed locally on your device. Game state (such as unlocked levels and scores) is stored securely using @capacitor/preferences and local fallback mechanisms. No personal information or data is uploaded, shared, or transmitted to external servers.
